AI Limitations · Human Edge

The Four Gaps AI Cannot Close.

GAP_01

No Context Understanding

AI doesn't understand the "why" behind code — not business logic, not customer needs, not the 3-year-old tech debt decision that shapes everything.

GAP_02

Complex Architecture

AI writes individual scripts well. But designing a full, interconnected system — backend, frontend, DevOps, security, scaling — still requires a human brain.

GAP_03

Accountability

When the system crashes at 3 AM, someone is responsible. You can't fire an AI. You can't sue it. The developer always owns the result.

GAP_04

Creative Solving

Real programming is communication and creativity — solving problems that were never on Stack Overflow. That requires a human with context, empathy, and judgment.
